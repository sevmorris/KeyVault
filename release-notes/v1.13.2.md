Quit works again while a prompt is open.

**Fixed**
- **⌘Q quits at launch.** KeyVault opens with the passphrase prompt, and while it, or any other sheet, alert or file picker, was open, macOS refused to quit the app with no sign of it. Closing the window then left KeyVault in the Dock, "Running in Background". The Dock's Quit, logging out and restarting were refused the same way. Quitting now always works, and anything left unsaved in an open sheet is discarded.

**Developer**
- Quit is a command of KeyVault's own, and the quit Apple Event has a handler of its own (`AppQuit.swift`). Each asks AppKit to terminate as usual and, when AppKit refuses because a sheet is open, finishes the quit itself.
