**Added**
- **Categories can be renamed and removed.** Organise Notes (⇧⌘O) now lists every category under the notes, each with how many notes are filed under it. Its menu renames it on all of them at once — so a misspelling is fixed in one step, and renaming onto a name already in use merges the two — or removes it, leaving those notes uncategorised. As with everything else in Organise Notes, nothing is written until Save.

**Fixed**
- **A misspelt category no longer lingers in the pickers after every note has left it.** The pickers listed what was stored rather than what was in front of you, so a category stayed on offer until Save.
- **A note that fails to file is now reported.** Saving in Organise Notes reported a failure and then cleared the report while reloading the list, so the sheet closed as if everything had worked and that note quietly kept its old category.
